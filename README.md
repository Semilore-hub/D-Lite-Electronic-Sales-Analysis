# D-Lite-Electronic-Sales-Analysis
## Sales analysis project using Excel dashboard and pivot table
## PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project analyzes electronic sales data to uncover trends, revenue performance and product profitability using excel
pivot tables and dashboard
## OBJECTIVES
  - Analyze total sales revenue
  - Identify top-selling products
  - Determine the most profitable category
  - Evaluate Monthly Sales performance
## DATA CLEANING
  - Removed duplicates
  - Checked for missing values
  - Created calculated columns
      - Reveue = unit sold * Unite price
## ANALYSIS TOOLS USED
  - Excel
  - Pivot Tables to summarize by Product, Category, and Month
  - Charts ( Bar, Line Pie) for visualization
  - Dashboard
## KEY INSIGHTS
   **Region Insight**
  - The Eastern Region generated the highest total revenue among all regions which indicated strong market performance
     and higher customer demand in that area
  - This suggest that the company shpould focus more on marketing efforts ad inventory supply in the eastern region to
     maximize profits
  - Although other region contributes greatly too but none outperformed the easten region in overall sales
    
  **Product Performance**
  - Phone X12 was the highest- selling product, which contributed to the large share of revenue
  - it indicates that there is a strong customer demand of this product
    
  **Sales representative Perfoemance**
  - Peter Obi has the top performing sales representative
  - he significantly contributed to the overall company revenue
  - the strategies he used can be replicated across other sales reps to improve there performances

  **Category Performance**
  - The computer category has the highest total sales
  - This shows that cutomers are more inclined toward computer-related products copared to other categories
  - the company should priortize so much in this category in marketing campaigns and stock planning

   **Total revenue Pefromance**
   - the total revenue that was generated by the company is #323,734,631
   - strategic focus on high performing products,regions and representatives can further increase profitability
